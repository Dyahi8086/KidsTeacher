Privacy Policy

Kids Teacher explains how information is handled when a parent or guardian uses this app.

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Information we use

  • Google account information made available during sign-in, such as the account name and email address.
  • The child name entered by the parent or guardian.
  • The Buddy character selected in the app and learning information that may be saved when related features are used.

How information is used

Information is used to sign users in, keep each user's learning profile separate, show the child's chosen name and Buddy, and provide app features.

Storage and service providers

Kids Teacher uses Google sign-in through Supabase Authentication. The app stores account profile information, including the parent account ID and email address, child name, selected Buddy, and learning progress when those features are used, in the Kids Teacher Supabase project. Google processes sign-in information under Google's applicable terms and privacy policy. Supabase processes authentication and database information under Supabase's applicable terms and privacy policy.

The app may keep account-related information in the browser or device storage to support the sign-in session and app experience. Do not use a shared device without signing out.

Children's information

Kids Teacher is intended to be used with a parent or legal guardian's involvement. A parent or guardian should provide the child's name, supervise use of the app, and avoid entering sensitive personal information about a child.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by Google for sign-in and Supabase for authentication and database hosting, as needed to operate the app.

Retention and deletion

Account and profile information may remain in the Supabase project until it is deleted by the app administrator. Signing out ends the local sign-in session but does not delete the cloud profile. To request deletion, contact the app owner through the Kids Teacher GitHub repository.

Security

The app uses account-based access controls intended to keep each user's profile separate. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as Kids Teacher changes. For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact the Kids Teacher app owner through the Kids Teacher GitHub repository.
