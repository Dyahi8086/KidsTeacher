Privacy Policy

Kids Teacher explains how information is handled when a parent or guardian uses this app.

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Information we use

  • Google account information made available during sign-in, such as the account name and email address.
  • The child name entered by the parent or guardian.
  • The Buddy character selected in the app and learning information that may be saved when related features are used.

How information is used

Information is used to sign users in, keep each user's learning profile separate, show the child's chosen name and Buddy, and provide app features.

Storage and service providers

Kids Teacher uses Google Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore to authenticate users and store profile information. These services process information under Google's applicable terms and privacy policies. The app may also store a small account-specific profile cache on the device to help the interface load.

Children's information

A parent or legal guardian should enter a child's name and supervise the child's use of the app. Please do not enter sensitive personal information.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information is processed by the service providers needed to operate the app, as described above.

Retention and deletion

Profile information remains in the Firebase project until it is deleted by the account owner or app administrator. Signing out clears the app's local profile cache on that device, but does not itself delete the cloud profile.

Security

Reasonable technical measures, including account-based access rules, are used to protect profile data. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as Kids Teacher changes. For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact the Kids Teacher app owner through the GitHub repository where this app is published.
